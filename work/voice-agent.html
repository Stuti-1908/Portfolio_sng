The brief

Outbound sales teams spend most of their time dialling people who don't answer. The goal was an autonomous pipeline that enriches leads, calls them, qualifies them and books meetings with no human in the loop, while staying TCPA-compliant.

CHALLENGE 01

Calls had to sound natural and handle objections in real time.

CHALLENGE 02

US calling regulations (TCPA) required consent-aware dialling.

CHALLENGE 03

Leads had to be prioritised before any call was placed.

Engineering decisions

Hard problems, solved

01

Score before dialling

LLM enrichment and classification mean agents call the most promising leads first.

02

03

Compliance by design

TCPA rules are built into the calling flow rather than left to operators.
