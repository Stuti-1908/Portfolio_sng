The brief

LevelUp Northeast 2026 was organised with the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) and the Indian Digital Gaming Society (IDGS). Registration day would bring thousands of users at once, and bulk athlete data arrived as messy CSV files.

CHALLENGE 01

A launch-day traffic spike could not be allowed to corrupt a single record.

CHALLENGE 02

Bulk CSV imports contained malformed rows and duplicates.

CHALLENGE 03

Certificates needed stable, collision-free IDs.

What I built

The product

Registration portal

React and Tailwind, deployed to Vercel and Azure Container Apps.

Fault-tolerant ingestion

Node.js CSV engine with concurrency control and dry-run validation.

Row recovery

Malformed rows are recovered instead of being dropped.

Deterministic IDs

64-bit certificate IDs that never collide.

Two-tier RAG search

Postgres tsvector + pgvector, with a Qdrant and Gemini fallback.

Scrum delivery

Delivered in sprints using Jira and Confluence.

Engineering decisions

Hard problems, solved

01

Dry run before commit

Every import is validated first, and only clean, reconciled data is committed.

02

Concurrency control

Locks on write paths kept the traffic spike from corrupting records.

03

Tiered retrieval

Cheap keyword and vector search answers most queries, and deep RAG runs only when needed.
