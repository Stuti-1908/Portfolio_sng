The brief

A Dubai welfare group's HR team was buried in manual screening, scheduling and onboarding. They needed a clear, safe plan to bring AI into the workflow, with people keeping the final say, and a team able to run it afterwards.

CHALLENGE 01

Hiring decisions are sensitive, so AI had to assist rather than decide.

CHALLENGE 02

The team had little prior exposure to AI or automation tools.

CHALLENGE 03

The blueprint had to be practical to maintain in-house.

Engineering decisions

Hard problems, solved

01

Assist, don't decide

Every AI step in a hiring decision ends at a human approval, which protects fairness and keeps accountability with the team.

02

Blueprint first

A complete written blueprint let leadership see the full workflow before anything was built.

03

Enablement as a deliverable

Coaching the internal team was part of the scope, so the organisation owns the system rather than renting a consultant.
