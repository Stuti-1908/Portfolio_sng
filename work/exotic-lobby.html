The brief

Exotic Lobby is an invitation-only community for owners of the world's rarest cars: Ferraris, McLarens, Lamborghinis and Porsches, with members across Europe, the Middle East and the US.

The client needed a premium product that felt worthy of its members: live convoys, private chat, events, garages and service booking. They also needed one engineer who could own everything from architecture to App Store delivery.

CHALLENGE 01

Real-time location for moving convoys, with chat, had to stay reliable on mobile networks across countries.

CHALLENGE 02

A third-party CDN was failing for international members, and heavy media was breaking the experience.

CHALLENGE 03

Different permissions were needed for members, community leaders and global admins, enforced at the database level.

Engineering decisions

Hard problems, solved

01

Security in the database

Row-level security policies in PostgreSQL mean a member can never read another member's private data, even if the client app is compromised.

02

Owning the media pipeline

I migrated 79 assets off a failing third-party CDN to Supabase storage, cutting payload from 68 MB to 14 MB and fixing access for international members.

03

One codebase, three platforms

React Native plus TypeScript let a single engineer ship and maintain iOS, Android and web without the quality gaps that usually come with that.
