The brief

Founders don't open coaching apps. They do answer texts. DadaAI wanted a coach that meets executives where they already are, remembers every commitment and follows up relentlessly.

I architected and launched the platform, and later rescued its automation layer by migrating it off a fragile vendor dependency with zero downtime.

CHALLENGE 01

Answers had to feel instant over SMS while drawing on each founder's full history.

CHALLENGE 02

The automation backbone depended on 43 Airtable nodes across 14 workflows and was becoming unstable.

CHALLENGE 03

Email, SMS and voice had to work as one coaching experience.

Engineering decisions

Hard problems, solved

01

Cache in front of vectors

Redis serves frequent context instantly and pgvector HNSW handles semantic recall, so retrieval stays under 500 ms.

02

Removing a single point of failure

I migrated 43 Airtable nodes across 14 production workflows to Supabase with zero downtime, stabilising 448+ automated client interactions.

03

Right model per task

A router picks GPT-4, Claude or Gemini per task, balancing quality, latency and cost.
